# Commercial Credit Policy

This fictional policy exists only for the CreditOps portfolio demonstration.

## 5.3 — Debt Service Requirements

Commercial borrowers must maintain a minimum Debt Service Coverage Ratio
of 1.25x.

Debt Service Coverage Ratio is calculated as EBITDA divided by annual debt
service.

A result below 1.25x is a potential covenant breach. The relationship must be
referred to an authorized credit officer for review. CreditOps must not approve,
waive, or amend the covenant.

## 5.4 — Leverage Requirements

Commercial borrowers must maintain total debt to EBITDA of no more than 3.00x,
unless a facility agreement contains a separately approved threshold.

A result above the applicable threshold must be recorded as a potential
covenant breach and referred for human review.

## 5.5 — Liquidity Requirements

Borrowers covered by the standard commercial policy must maintain a current
ratio of at least 1.20x.

## 6.2 — Human Decision Authority

Automated systems may retrieve evidence, calculate ratios, identify exceptions,
and prepare draft relationship reviews.

Only an authorized credit officer may approve credit, waive a covenant, amend a
facility, or make a final lending decision.
